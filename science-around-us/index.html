THINK BEYOND THE HEADLINE

How to Read a Discovery

Not every exciting headline means a discovery is final. A few questions can help you explore science more carefully.

01

Find the Original Source

Open the original article, mission page or research paper. Check who published it and when it appeared.

02

Look at the Evidence

Ask what researchers observed, measured or tested. Separate the actual result from possible explanations.

03

Ask What Comes Next

Is the result preliminary? Does it need independent confirmation? What new questions does it raise?
