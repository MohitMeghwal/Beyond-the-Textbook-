01 · VOLTAGE

Pushes the charges

Voltage provides the electrical potential difference that drives charge through the circuit.

02 · RESISTANCE

Opposes current

Resistance opposes the flow of electric current. Increasing resistance reduces current when voltage stays constant.

03 · CURRENT

The result

According to Ohm's Law, current is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance.

💡

Don't memorize V = IR. Test it.

Keep resistance constant and increase voltage. Watch the current increase. Then keep voltage constant and increase resistance. Observe the current decrease.
