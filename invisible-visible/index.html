Look Beyond What You See

Reality is full of invisible processes.

Your eyes show you the world. Science reveals what is happening inside it.

Electric current is not a visible stream of tiny balls. A magnetic field cannot normally be seen. Sound travels through invisible changes in pressure. Even inside your cells, millions of molecular events happen every second. Here, we turn these hidden ideas into visuals you can explore.

How Do Scientists See the Invisible?

Science uses evidence, models and instruments.

01 — OBSERVE

Notice the Effect

We may not see the cause directly, but we can observe what it does.

02 — MODEL

Build a Model

Scientists use diagrams, equations, simulations and physical models to represent hidden processes.

03 — TEST

Test the Idea

If the model correctly predicts what happens, it becomes a useful scientific explanation.
